# YUNU

### AI-native platforms for commerce, media, and intelligent digital experiences.

**YUNU LTDA** is a Brazilian technology company focused on designing and building software platforms where artificial intelligence is part of the operating architecture, not an accessory added afterward.

## What we build

YUNU develops intelligent platforms that bring together software architecture, domain intelligence, automation, and AI-driven decision systems.

Our current product initiatives span two complementary areas:

### YUNU.COMMERCE

**AI-native commerce and catalog intelligence.**

A platform focused on catalog intelligence, taxonomy resolution, enrichment, data quality, semantic understanding, and intelligent integration across commerce ecosystems.

**Stage:** Private development

### YUNU.MEDIA

**AI-native media and intelligent content distribution.**

A media platform being designed around intelligent content understanding, automated cataloging, recommendation, distribution, safety, creator experiences, monetization, and AI-assisted platform operations.

**Stage:** Architecture and product definition

## Engineering principles

We design platforms around a small set of durable principles:

- **AI-native by design**
- **Domain-driven architecture**
- **Provider-neutral AI integration**
- **Human governance for high-impact automated decisions**
- **Security, privacy, and observability by default**
- **Scalable distributed systems**
- **Clear architectural boundaries and explicit contracts**
- **Documentation and architecture before implementation**
